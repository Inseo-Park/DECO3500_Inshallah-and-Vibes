# Team decisions and working sessions

The team developed ECHO through domain selection, research, parallel concept exploration, physical prototyping and successive social-garden iterations.

## Decisions and iterations

| Date | Team decisions and development |
| --- | --- |
| 2026-08-17–18 | The team compared UQ history, marine biology, cooking and routine-related domains, then narrowed the choice to cooking and UQ history. Keerthana confirmed the selection of history. Ishan began drafting interview questions following advice to investigate stakeholder values and experiences. |
| 2026-08-22–23 | Keerthana proposed analysing interviews alongside secondary research, generating concepts and updating the presentation. Inseo removed participant names, while Itisha revised the slides to connect research insights to the project context and added citations. |
| 2026-08-27 | The team divided initial concept exploration between scavenger hunt (Inseo/Alex), then/now/next (Itisha), UQ board game (Keerthana) and stories of UQ (Ishan). Itisha also proposed a story-capturing and storytelling variation. |
| 2026-09-01–08 | Ishan coordinated prototype uploads and requested student feedback on concept preferences and reasons. Ishan added UQ Stories feedback, and Keerthana added the board-game feedback, questionnaire and responses. |
| 2026-09-10–17 | The team moved towards a pod-like physical form while continuing to develop its features. Discussion then focused on materials, the physical model and testing the complete book experience before presenting it. |
| 2026-09-18–21 | Itisha identified that the physical design obstructed opening and closing the book and shared user-testing results. Inseo checked additional prototype functions on the received device. Keerthana encountered an LED-strip wiring setback caused by a missing cable; trying another microcontroller did not resolve it. |
| 2026-10-08 | Inseo shared an AI-assisted, two-participant HTML exploration using separate clues, joint decisions and a shared poster. Ishan raised concerns that the interface could still be completed individually and that users needed a way to return to their echoes. Keerthana and Itisha favoured developing social interaction within the existing application, including connections between users at different book locations. |
| 2026-10-09 | Keerthana shared an exploration where an echo becomes a flower on a tree and reactions make it bloom. Ishan shared a separate two-person painting and memory exploration using shared paint and joint commenting. These alternatives explored ways to make contributions and collaboration visible. |
| 2026-10-11 | Itisha circulated Minecraft-style and normal-garden versions for comparison. Team review identified unclear notifications and comment labels, incorrect flower zoom, editing and deletion needs, and difficulty finding one's own tree. Itisha proposed taking the Minecraft-style version to tutor review. |
| 2026-10-11–12 | Ishan gathered an informal comparison from four people. Feedback favoured the Minecraft-style version and suggested self-paced onboarding, clearer navigation and notifications, easier access to one's own tree, richer conversation and attention to outdoor use conditions. This comparison focused on version preference and social usability. Keerthana identified historical learning and interest in UQ history as separate evaluation needs; the comparison did not measure learning outcomes. |
| 2026-10-12–13 | Itisha shared a revised voxel prototype with player-like avatars and conversation in response to feedback. The proposed experience connected book locations, live users, trees and anonymous interaction. |
| 2026-10-15 | The team clarified that the garden and trees would be shared across book locations while the local monument changed. Users should be able to communicate across locations and access their tree from any book. Ishan and Keerthana proposed a tutorial to explain these interactions. |
| 2026-10-16–18 | The team reviewed and revised the poster alongside prototype development, brochure revision and printing preparation. Keerthana shared an edited brochure. Review included checking whether poster claims matched implemented and simulated behaviour. |
| 2026-10-19 | The team investigated missing contribution flowers. Ishan proposed a fix; Itisha identified integration and media problems with that version and shared a further revision with contribution animation and notifications. Ishan confirmed a flower after refreshing. Inseo found lag on both iPad location variants and later observed improvement with some lag remaining. Itisha shared local-network setup instructions, and Ishan reported that live conversation worked. A refresh requirement remained; these checks did not confirm that all device, flower or synchronisation issues were resolved in v22. |
| 2026-10-20–22 | Following the exhibit, Keerthana outlined feedback topics. Ishan created issues for remaining submission work. The team coordinated collecting the book and sharing exhibit images and video. |
