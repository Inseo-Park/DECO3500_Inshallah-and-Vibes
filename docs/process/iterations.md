# Iteration record

ECHO developed from early campus-history concepts into a book-like storytelling installation and shared social garden. The [team decisions](team-records.md) describe the working sessions and choices behind these changes.

| Stage | Findings and team decisions | Design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposal and parallel concepts, August–early September | The team selected UQ history and explored four directions through [initial research](../research-summary.md), [early prototypes](../proposal/low-fidelity/) and [feedback](../proposal/feedback/). | Explored campus history through stories, games, scavenging and visual concepts, with short, optional interactions as a central goal. |
| Early ECHO / book form, September | [Weeks 6–8 deck](week-09/weeks-06-08-iterations.pdf), [session summary](week-09/echo-sessions.md), [Wizard-of-Oz evidence](week-09/wizard-of-oz-statistics.csv), [interviews](week-09/interview-quotes.md). | Explored listening/sharing and physical approachability. Feedback supported format choice, short content and comfortable placement. A decline in users reaching later logged stages is evidence to investigate, not proof of a single cause. |
| Stronger social interaction, 8–9 October | The team identified that the experience could still be completed individually and explored two-person activities and a flower/tree contribution model. | Developed options for joint activities and connecting visitors at different books. These remained separate explorations rather than a single completed feature set. |
| Realistic versus voxel gardens, 11 October | The team compared the [realistic-tree prototype](../../archive/prototypes/echo-social/index.html) and [Minecraft-style prototype](../../archive/prototypes/echo-social-minecraft/index.html), supported by [feedback from four participants](../evaluation/social-garden-feedback.md). | Navigation and overlapping trees favoured investigating the voxel direction. Only P2 explicitly stated an overall preference in the written feedback. Jitter, zoom, blank-area clicks and finding one's own tree remained concerns. |
| Avatars, conversation and location model, 12–15 October | The team developed avatar and conversation revisions and clarified a shared garden with location-specific landmarks. | Strengthened awareness and conversation across locations. Earlier usability issues still needed checking in the revised build. |
| Device checks and exhibit preparation, 16–20 October | The team reviewed promotional material, investigated iPad lag and missing flowers, configured the relay and checked live conversation. | Live conversation worked in a team check, while residual lag and a flower-refresh requirement remained. The [final poster and brochures](../exhibition/) support the exhibition presentation. |
| Current prototype: echo-tradeshow | [echo-tradeshow](../../prototype/index.html) brings together the two campus-location variants and shared-garden interface. | Includes Lakes/Court variants and a dependency-free relay. [Current limitations](../prototype-guide.md) include browser-local persistence, English-only UI and unwired Echo transmission. Final user testing is still needed. |

## Evidence still needed

The next evaluation should check the latest build on two actual exhibit devices: whether visitors understand the location relationship, can find their tree and return to a conversation, can use the garden without accidental selection/zoom, and can discover and discuss campus history. Record device/browser details, observed task outcomes and participants' accounts separately. Include the unresolved Echo propagation and persistence limits in the test setup.

October feedback recommendations still need implementation and retesting.
