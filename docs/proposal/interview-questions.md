Domain:
Educating UQ students about the cultural, political, architectural and topographical history of their campus, so they can properly acknowledge the land and its people. 

Users: 
First and second Semester students (Induviduals who have spent first sem settling into the new environment, get jobs, etc and may not have time to explore yet) 
University students who lack the feeling of belongingness
University students who are interested about campus history

Interview Questions:
Can you tell us an intersting fact you discovered about the campus ? 
Tell me something interesting  about the history of UQ and how did you find out about it? (will help understand where people get their history about  UQ from now) 
When you visit a new place, what are the kind of things you are most excited to learn about ? 
Describe a learning experience where you felt engaged and connected to the material? 
Tell me about the last time you talked to someone back home about uni. What did you end up telling them about? 
Have you ever visited any of an indigenous architecture in UQ? What was it and how did you think of the place? 
What was the first place on campus that started to feel like the campus was yours? How did that happen? 
If you had to pick one specific spot or hidden historical corner on campus that feels most meaningful to you, what happened there to make it special, and what does it tell you about the kind of community UQ has been over time?
