What's an Echo?

An Echo is something someone has left behind — a story, a memory, a photo, a moment — for someone else to find.

Someone was here before you.
Now you get to find what they left.

Watch, listen, read — or swipe through all of it in Reel mode. Then leave something of your own for whoever sits here next.

In this prototype, the people you see reading at the other ECHOes are simulated. Two ECHOes can be linked live from the garden menu.

Which ECHO is this?

There are ECHOes in seven places. Each one opens the place it stands in; the rest of the island stays under cloud until you visit them. A story can carry on at another ECHO.
