# ECHO: Week 9 stand-up prototype

This is the prototype used for the Week 9 stand-up. It was designed for an iPad in portrait mode. Visitors can browse video, audio, written stories and photos, then leave a contribution.

## Open it

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser. Keep the `media/` folder beside it so the recordings and pictures load. No installation or internet connection is needed for the bundled content.

For the latest prototype, use [echo-tradeshow](../../../prototype/index.html).

## Export session records

Hold the small round button at the bottom right of the first or last screen for about a second. On a keyboard, press **Ctrl+Shift+D** on Windows or **Cmd+Shift+D** on macOS.

Choose **Save CSV** to export the session records. They include the screens visited, formats chosen and recorded interactions. Records and comments stay in that browser. Export what you need before clearing its data.

## Limits

- The language menu lists other languages, but the interface stays in English.
- The subtitles button shows a placeholder. Subtitles are not included.
- Voice recording depends on browser support and microphone permission. It may be blocked when the page is opened as a local file.
- Example names, dates, captions and comments include invented content. The written archive stories are loosely based on UQ protest history and should not be treated as verified historical accounts.
- Contributions are not published or shared between devices.
