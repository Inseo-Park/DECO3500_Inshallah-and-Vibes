What's an Echo?

An Echo is something someone has left behind — a story, a memory, a photo, a moment — for someone else to find.

Someone was here before you.
Now you get to find what they left.

Watch, listen, read — or swipe through all of it in Reel mode. Then leave something of your own for whoever sits here next.

In this prototype, the other people you see reading at the pods are simulated — the pods aren’t connected to each other yet.

Which ECHO is this?

There are pods in four places. They hold different Echoes, and a story can carry on at another one. For the prototype you can move this iPad between them.
